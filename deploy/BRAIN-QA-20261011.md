## Direct retrieval follow-up

The direct public-source adapter was subsequently tested on the same isolated Germany runtime. Phone research returned `ready` in 10,066 ms with both reviewed credit and BNPL URLs and a retrieval timestamp, using only one OpenAI response request and no paid web-search request. The answer distinguished the two provider-advertised paths and did not claim personal approval. This closes the no-evidence failure for this tested goods/mobile scenario, not for all product categories. The answer unnecessarily asked whether to use Toman/Rial despite explicit Toman input; the policy now tells the model to preserve known units and avoid repeated questions. That final prompt refinement is locally tested/build-verified, not separately live-verified.
