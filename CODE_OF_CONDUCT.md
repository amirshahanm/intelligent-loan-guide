# Code of Conduct

We want project participation to be useful, respectful, and technically focused.

Contributors should discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, give specific and actionable review feedback, respect privacy, avoid exposing another person's identifying or financial information, and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, or deliberate publication of sensitive data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or users.
